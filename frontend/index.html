About the role

n8n Lagos is looking for a People Operations Analyst to help our growing team make better decisions and run smoother employee processes. You will work across People, Finance and department leads to improve data quality, reporting and the workflows behind key moments in the employee journey.

This role suits someone who enjoys moving between careful analysis and practical process improvement. You do not need to know every HR platform on day one, but you should be comfortable learning systems, documenting your work and communicating clearly.

What you will do

  • Maintain accurate employee data and support regular data-quality checks.
  • Prepare headcount, turnover and operational reports for People and business leaders.
  • Document and improve workflows such as onboarding, employee changes and offboarding.
  • Support HRIS and ATS administration, testing and user requests.
  • Partner with stakeholders to clarify requirements and resolve process issues.
  • Identify appropriate opportunities for low-risk workflow automation.

What we are looking for

  • At least two years in HR operations, people analytics, HRIS support or a closely related role.
  • Practical spreadsheet and data-analysis experience.
  • Evidence of process documentation or workflow improvement.
  • Clear written communication and confidence working with different stakeholders.
  • Care with confidential information and good judgment about data access.

Helpful, but not required

  • Experience administering an HRIS or applicant tracking system.
  • Dashboard or workforce-reporting experience.
  • Exposure to workflow automation tools.
